How WhiteBridge AI Works

At a high level, the digital identity research workflow functions similarly to an open-web search engine in three general steps:

🌐

1. Public Data Collection

The service identifies and collects information strictly from publicly accessible online sources, open directories, and web pages.

🔗

2. Linking & Grouping

Matching public identifiers (such as common names or public usernames) found across open platforms are grouped together.

📊

3. AI Report Organization

The resulting information can be organized into a structured digital summary for easier research.

🛡️

Public Scope Only

WhiteBridge explicitly states that it functions like a search engine, only looks at what is already public, and never accesses private accounts or hidden sources.
